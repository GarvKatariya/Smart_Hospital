# User Guide

## For Patients
1. **Dashboard**: View upcoming appointments and past medical reports.
2. **Symptom Checker**: Navigate to the 'Symptom Checker' tab. Describe your symptoms in the text box or select them from the list. The AI will predict potential conditions and recommend a hospital department.
3. **Disease Risk**: Under 'Assessments', enter your medical metrics (blood pressure, cholesterol, etc.) to evaluate heart disease risk.
4. **Document Upload**: Upload prescriptions or lab reports to have them summarized automatically.

## For Doctors
1. **Patient Management**: View your assigned patients, their AI-summarized histories, and predicted risk factors.
2. **Triage Assistant**: When a new patient arrives, the Triage module will highlight urgent cases based on their vitals.
3. **Prescription Parsing**: Quickly review digital extractions of uploaded handwritten notes.

## For Administrators
1. **Bed Forecasting**: Use the Admin Dashboard to view a 30-day forecast of bed occupancy to manage staffing and admissions.
2. **User Management**: Grant doctor/admin privileges to staff accounts.
3. **System Logs**: Monitor AI prediction confidences and system usage.
