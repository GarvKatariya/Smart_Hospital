# Hospital Services

We provide comprehensive medical services:
- **Emergency**: 24/7 care for urgent medical issues, accidents, and trauma.
- **OPD (Outpatient Department)**: Scheduled consultations with specialists.
- **IPD (Inpatient Department)**: Hospitalization and round-the-clock nursing care.
- **Diagnostic Services**: X-Rays, MRI, CT Scans, Ultrasound, Pathology Lab.
- **Pharmacy**: In-house 24/7 pharmacy for all prescription needs.
- **Ambulance**: 24/7 transport services.
