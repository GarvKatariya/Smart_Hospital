# Emergency Department Procedures

- **When to visit ER**: Severe bleeding, chest pain, breathing difficulty, loss of consciousness, severe trauma, sudden weakness or numbness.
- **Triage Process**: Upon arrival, patients are assessed (triaged) based on severity. Critical cases (Emergency/High) are treated immediately regardless of arrival time.
- **What to bring**: Identification, insurance details, current medications list.
- **Contact**: In case of life-threatening emergencies requiring an ambulance, call our emergency hotline: 555-0911.
