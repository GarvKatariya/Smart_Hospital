# Billing and Insurance

- **Insurance**: We accept all major insurance providers. Please present your insurance card at the time of admission or registration.
- **Billing Process**: Bills are generated at the time of discharge for IPD patients, and after consultation for OPD patients.
- **Payment Methods**: We accept Cash, Credit/Debit Cards, UPI, and Bank Transfers.
- **Financial Assistance**: Available for eligible patients. Please contact the billing department at 555-0199 for more info.
