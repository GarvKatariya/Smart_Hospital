# Visiting Hours Policy

- **General Wards**: 4:00 PM to 7:00 PM daily. Only two visitors allowed per patient at a time.
- **Private Rooms**: 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM daily. Only two visitors allowed per patient at a time.
- **ICU/CCU**: 11:00 AM to 11:30 AM and 5:00 PM to 5:30 PM daily. Only one family member allowed per patient. No children under 12 allowed.
- **Pediatric Ward**: Parents can stay 24/7. Other visitors strictly during general visiting hours.
