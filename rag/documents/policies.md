# Hospital Policies

- **Privacy**: Patient information is strictly confidential and protected according to standard healthcare data privacy regulations.
- **Infection Control**: Hand hygiene is mandatory. Masks may be required in specific wards.
- **Complaints**: We value your feedback. Complaints can be submitted to the Patient Experience Office on the ground floor.
- **No Smoking**: The entire hospital campus is a strict no-smoking zone.
