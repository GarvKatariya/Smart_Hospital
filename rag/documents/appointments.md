# Appointments Policy

- **Booking**: Appointments can be booked through our online portal, via phone at 555-0100, or in-person at the OPD reception.
- **Cancellation**: Please cancel at least 24 hours in advance to avoid a no-show fee.
- **Rescheduling**: Can be done up to 12 hours before the appointment.
- **No-Show Policy**: Three consecutive no-shows may lead to a temporary restriction on online booking.
- **Wait Times**: Please arrive 15 minutes before your scheduled slot. Walk-in patients will be seen based on availability after scheduled patients.
